# Developmental roadmap: 30–72 months

This map is a product starting point, not a developmental test. Children often show uneven profiles and may use content from adjacent bands.

## Band A — 30–36 months

### Typical product assumptions

A child may recognize at least one color, follow simple routines and two-step instructions, use pretend play, turn pages and manipulate objects. The interface should assume limited reading, short attention and developing fine motor control.

### Suitable mechanics

- identical matching and shadow matching;
- two-way sorting by color or shape;
- big/small comparisons;
- one-to-one distribution;
- hiding under 2–3 locations;
- 1–2 item sequences;
- wait-for-signal and simple go/no-go;
- two-frame daily routines;
- large inset puzzles and wide tracing;
- basic emotion matching;
- parent-guided color hunt.

### Limits

- 2–3 answer choices;
- one rule;
- no time pressure;
- no small drag targets;
- no rotation required;
- audio instruction under roughly one sentence;
- adult present or immediately available.

## Band B — 3–4 years

### Product direction

Expand vocabulary, causal order, simple patterns, 2–3 item memory, category knowledge and controlled rule changes with visible cues.

### Suitable mechanics

- semantic sorting;
- odd-one-out;
- visual search;
- AB/AAB/ABB patterns;
- 2–4 step story order;
- 3–4 location memory;
- 2–3 item light or sound sequences;
- simple maze and route choice;
- “more/less” up to small quantities;
- descriptions with two attributes;
- simple social-help scenarios;
- copying real block models.

### Limits

- rule remains visible;
- repeat audio is always available;
- one distractor at most in sequence tasks;
- open-ended play remains unscored.

## Band C — 4–5 years

### Product direction

Introduce flexible sorting, planning, symmetry, mental manipulation, quantity-symbol links, simple operations with objects and richer narrative reasoning.

### Suitable mechanics

- switching between color and shape;
- 3–4 item memory sequences;
- multiple missing pattern positions;
- route comparison and 2–3 step lookahead;
- tangram with partial outlines;
- symmetry completion;
- quantities up to 10 and number ordering;
- composing a number from groups;
- first-sound matching;
- short story comprehension;
- perspective in simple social scenes.

### Limits

- do not convert every game into speed;
- symbolic number tasks stay connected to manipulatives;
- social scenarios allow discussion and may have more than one reasonable response.

## Band D — 5–6 years

### Product direction

Increase multi-step planning, conflict control, working-memory span, phonological awareness, early arithmetic stories and simple algorithmic thinking.

### Suitable mechanics

- 4–6 item sequences where tolerated;
- rule switches with cue only at the switch;
- conflict cards and stop-signal tasks;
- mental rotation;
- 4–6 command routes on grids;
- ABC/growing patterns;
- concrete addition and subtraction within small ranges;
- rhyme matching;
- story with two events and inferential question;
- create and follow a simple real-world map.

### Limits

- no school-like grading;
- no percentile or age comparison;
- no forced advancement;
- allow return to concrete objects and visual support.

## Domain progression

| Domain | 30–36m | 3–4y | 4–5y | 5–6y |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Visual attention | identical, contour | search, missing piece | differences, orientation | denser scenes, controlled similarity |
| Classification | one visible rule | semantic categories | two features | overlapping categories |
| Working memory | 1–2 items/locations | 2–3 | 3–4 | 4–6 if successful |
| Inhibition | wait, simple no-go | freeze/move | opposite and switch | conflict and stop signal |
| Sequencing | first/then | 3–4 story frames | cause and pattern rules | algorithms and growing patterns |
| Spatial | inset fit | simple maze | symmetry/tangram | rotation and grid planning |
| Numeracy | one-to-one, 1–3 | more/less, 1–5 | ordering/composition to 10 | concrete operations |
| Language | listen/find | attributes/categories | first sound, narrative | rhyme and inference |
| Social | basic emotions | helping choices | perspective cues | context and ambiguity |
| Motor/creative | wide trace, drag | simple construction | connect/model | symmetry and constrained creation |

## Placement rule

1. Start at the child’s age band.
2. Present two very easy demonstrations.
3. If the child needs repeated physical guidance, step one mechanic down.
4. If three clean successes occur across at least two sessions, advance one axis.
5. Keep skill domains independent.
6. Parent can manually lower difficulty; raising beyond the recommended band requires parent mode.
