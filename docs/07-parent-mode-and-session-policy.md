# Parent mode and session policy

## Parent gate

All settings, data controls, external information and additional-session actions are behind an adult-level task.

Recommended pattern:

- long press with two fingers for three seconds;
- then a written instruction and simple adult arithmetic or reading task;
- timeout after inactivity;
- child mode never displays an external URL.

A parental gate is not equivalent to legal parental consent for data collection. The product avoids that issue by not collecting online child data.

## Parent setup

Minimum setup:

- optional local display name;
- broad age band or optional birth month/year;
- Romanian/English;
- audio and music;
- reduced motion/high contrast;
- large/extra-large targets and normal/slower demonstrations;
- session limit: 3, 5 or 7 minutes;
- co-play prompts on/off;
- motor accommodation: large targets, generous drag, trace assistance.

Do not ask for:

- email;
- phone;
- exact address;
- school;
- photo;
- voice;
- medical/developmental diagnosis;
- contacts;
- location.

## Session defaults

- 30–36 months: 3 games, approximately 5–7 minutes.
- 3–4 years: 4 games, approximately 5–8 minutes.
- 4–6 years: 4–5 games, approximately 7–10 minutes.

These are product defaults, not medical prescriptions. The child can stop earlier and the parent limit always wins.

## Parent dashboard

Show:

- recently practiced domains;
- examples of mastered mechanics;
- hints trend;
- current difficulty in plain language;
- suggested real-world activity;
- session duration;
- data delete/export.

Do not show:

- intelligence score;
- developmental age;
- percentile;
- comparison with peers;
- “behind/ahead” labels;
- daily streak;
- red deficit charts.

Suggested wording:

- “Potrivește ușor 3 imagini diferite.”
- “Exersează încă secvențe de 3 elemente.”
- “Indiciile au fost folosite mai rar în ultimele sesiuni.”
- “Încercați aceeași idee cu cuburi reale.”

## Co-play prompt sequence

Before game:

- “Lasă-l să încerce câteva secunde înainte să ajuți.”

During game:

- “Întreabă: «Cum ai știut?»”
- “Arată o singură dată, apoi retrage ajutorul.”
- “Acceptă o pauză.”

After game:

- “Găsiți două obiecte similare în cameră.”
- “Refaceți secvența cu palmele.”
- “Sortați trei obiecte reale.”

## Stop signals

End immediately when:

- child says or signals no;
- turns away repeatedly;
- becomes visibly distressed;
- begins hitting/throwing device;
- session cap is reached;
- adult chooses stop.

The event is not recorded as a failed attempt.

## Data controls

Parent mode provides:

- export JSON/CSV to a file chosen by the parent;
- delete all progress;
- reset one domain;
- inspect app version/content version;
- privacy summary;
- licenses and source acknowledgements.

Export is manual and local. No automatic upload.
