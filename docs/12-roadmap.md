# Product roadmap

## R0 — specification repository

Current repository:

- evidence and claim limits;
- 80 game families;
- age-band map;
- level/adaptation design;
- pure TypeScript core;
- sample generators;
- schemas and validators;
- Codex instructions.

## R1 — technical prototype

- mobile shell;
- same-picture vertical slice;
- local profile;
- session cap;
- parent gate;
- airplane-mode pass.

## R2 — starter application

- 15 P0 games;
- 8 reusable archetypes;
- Romanian narration;
- accessibility;
- parent dashboard;
- real-world transfer.

## R3 — 3–4 years expansion

- semantic classification;
- more memory and patterns;
- story order;
- gentle mazes;
- descriptions and social helping.

## R4 — 4–5 years expansion

- rule switching;
- symmetry and tangram;
- quantity-symbol links;
- first sounds;
- richer narratives.

## R5 — 5–6 years expansion

- conflict control;
- algorithmic routes;
- mental rotation;
- concrete operations;
- rhyme and inference.

## Deferred by design

- cloud sync;
- school/classroom accounts;
- social sharing;
- child-generated online content;
- subscriptions/IAP;
- ads;
- third-party analytics;
- voice recognition;
- camera-based games;
- generative AI in the child runtime.

Any deferred capability requires a separate privacy, safety and value review.
